To infinity and beyond

I am an undergraduate at IIT Indore, and almost everything I have built lives in the air. Autonomous delivery drones, a light-following nano quad, a tethered charging platform, a lawnmower with an arm on it.

I like the part of the work where the simulation ends: soldering the harness, tuning the loop, walking the field with a transmitter and finding out what the model got wrong. My comfort zone spans flight controllers, companion computers, perception and control, and I lead teams through competitions where all of that has to work on one specific morning.
